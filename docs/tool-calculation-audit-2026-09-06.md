# Tool calculation release audit

Reviewed September 6, 2026, America/Chicago. The machine-readable inventory and exact guide list are in `tool-calculation-audit-2026-09-06.json`. This is source and test evidence, not a production deployment or a transaction receipt.

## Coverage and release gate

All **86 published free tools** are classified: **62 arithmetic/checklist tools** have independent reference examples; **24 document/link generators** have a separate classification and existing generator/export checks. Every tool runs the shared numeric-boundary checks, including its default input, each numeric field's supported edges, negatives, NaN, infinities and oversized values. A newly published unclassified tool fails the coverage check. Every shipped industry preset is also checked against its declared input domain. A successful build alone is not the evidence for the arithmetic.

`npm run validate:calculations` runs the reference cases, invariant/export checks, existing formula tests, debt regressions, guide accuracy tests, generator draft checks and paid-kit calculation tests. Both `npm run build` and `npm run build:only` invoke it before Next builds. Run this gate after formula, field, preset, formatting or export changes. Do not override the deployment build command with a bare `next build`.

The invoice-like schedules are reconciled in integer cents. Tests independently check **576 payment-plan combinations**, **72 amortization scenarios** with every monthly payment/interest/principal/balance row, and **42 inclusive/exclusive tax pairs**. An independent discounted-cash-flow search checks the loan payment without reusing the implementation's closed-form payment expression. Additional checks cover tax-year caps, wages, Medicare thresholds, five-minute timing, percentage ceilings, zero payback and denominators, duplicate selections, residual labor raises, unit-price precision, review averages, and debt beyond the 600-month computation window.

The common guard rejects unsupported numeric input before document or CSV exports can be generated. Supported money/number inputs are finite, nonnegative and at most 1 trillion; sliders use their declared limits, with integer values required where the step is 1. Option fields must use actual options. A second result guard refuses nonfinite outputs or amounts beyond safe cent precision, including when individually valid inputs multiply into an unsafe result. These are supported-model bounds, not assertions that extreme real-world scenarios are financially meaningful. Result examples do not certify tax, lending, insurance, payroll or platform compliance.

At source handoff, the calculation gate passes **328 tests in 24 suites**, the current full suite passes **888 tests in 88 suites**, and **22 article/OG/date/queue tests** pass. The additional paid-kit agent checks are automatically included through `tests/pro-kit*.test.ts` when they land. Typecheck passed; focused ESLint has zero errors and two expected image-element warnings in OG rendering. Parent owns the final integrated build and live verification.

## Verified defects corrected

- Payment plans formerly repeated a rounded installment, which could under/over-collect cents. The final payment now carries the remainder, the exact total is visible, and copy states that the fee applies to the full price before calculating the deposit.
- Loan schedules now show every monthly principal, interest, payment and balance amount. Interest uses annual-rate multiplication before division to avoid a floating-point half-cent rounding defect; the final payment clears the balance and totals reconcile.
- Sales-tax output conserves the inclusive total and separately rounds exclusive tax. The tax label describes the entered-rate calculation rather than claiming it is the actual tax legally due.
- Hire-versus-automation escalates residual human labor on both sides and deducts recurring system cost when estimating payback. Nonpositive savings has no positive payback.
- Vehicle cash outlay includes loan payments; economic cost includes depreciation and interest. They are separate selectable bases, avoiding principal/depreciation double counting.
- Lead and quote improvements stop at 100%. Cost-per-customer rejects customers exceeding same-cohort inquiries. Zero customers, zero spending, zero sales and other impossible ratio denominators no longer become plausible zero-cost or positive-profit results.
- The reply-speed comparison now uses exactly 5/60 hour instead of .08 hour. The scheduled September 8 insurance guide's illustrative annual display was corrected to $3,236 while preserving its future publication date and historical receipt.
- Meeting alternatives preserve preparation time and cannot lengthen an already shorter meeting. Per-seat unused licenses cannot exceed paid seats. Fractional expected transaction counts use the actual denominator for per-transaction cost.
- Debt payoff retains all accrued interest in a partial last payment. A flat balance at payment-equals-interest is distinct from increasing debt; a payoff beyond 50 years is distinct from never repaying.
- Review-average restoration avoids floating-point overcounts and handles impossible perfect averages. Arbitrary rating-to-lead/revenue figures were removed entirely. Website Scorecard also no longer invents a dollar loss from unchecked boxes.
- Small grocery unit prices retain fractional-cent precision. An already-funded reserve needs zero additional months. Zero essential expenses cannot establish months of reserve coverage.
- Review and job-post generators no longer invent credentials, completed work, automatic personal pickup, a desired rating, or other unsupported facts. Related metadata and applicable article instructions were brought into line with the corrected tools.

## What remains a scenario rather than a verified forecast

Formula correctness and predictive validity are different. The reply-speed, website-speed and form-length curves remain explicitly labeled **illustrative and unvalidated** in both result labels and notes; their exponential factors and floors do not establish real revenue lost or earned. Review impact and the website checklist have no dollar-loss model.

Acquisition payback uses steady assumed gross profit and a constant-churn model. Zero churn uses an explicitly labeled 60-month comparison horizon, not a discovered customer lifetime. Lead/lifetime-value referral assumptions do not recurse indefinitely. The 33% and 40% acquisition allocations are labeled arbitrary scenarios. Gross contribution is not net profit or cash available to spend.

Time-valued work and automation scenarios do not promise cash savings. The full-automation example is an upper bound: all entered task time/errors disappear, no residual labor is assumed, and error costs must exclude labor already counted. Owner compensation per hour is an average, not a marginal return from delegation. Conversion improvements are supplied assumptions, not measured causal effects or zero-cost work.

Loan and debt tools use simplified monthly interest rather than a lender's daily accrual, contractual fees or payment timing. The tax reserve uses a flat income-tax scenario rather than progressive brackets, credits, deductions, other income, safe harbors or required quarterly installments. The mileage output is a deduction estimate, not a tax credit or refund. Users must supply actual units and appropriate periods.
