## What the acquisition page sells

The page sells one complete outcome: a business owner brings a laptop and a real task, learns how ChatGPT works in plain English, sees a real business asset built, and leaves with a clear first use case.

The full service ladder does not appear on the acquisition page. The only sales-disclosure line is:

### The AI Business Clinic

The clinic protects Ryan's time while making sure no attendee feels overlooked.

- All attendees submit one business bottleneck.
- All attendees receive a Next Move card with one use case, one recommended tool, and one next action.
- Two businesses receive 12-minute live hot seats.
- Ryan uses the final six minutes to summarize the patterns that apply to the whole room.

This should be included for the first cohort. After proof and testimonials exist, it can become a $47 add-on or a higher-ticket workshop benefit.

## Recurring event model

Do not promise Tuesday and Thursday every week until the first cohort proves the format. The operating sequence should be:

1. Run one founding workshop with 6 to 10 attendees.
2. Record reactions, questions, objections, and completion rate.
3. Collect honest reviews and permissioned testimonials.
4. Tighten the curriculum and demo.
5. Open one Tuesday and one Thursday date at a time.
6. Move the rate up after the founding sessions.

At full capacity, one workshop produces $970 in ticket revenue. Two full workshops per week produce $1,940, or $7,760 over four weeks before expenses. The workshop is the front-end trust and qualification engine. The System Map, Implementation Sprint, and Company OS are what make the larger September revenue goal possible.
