## Lead and email route

1. Meta saves the Instant Form submission.
2. The signed Meta webhook pulls the lead into the LeadFlow Pro lead table. A five-minute backfill job recovers anything the webhook misses.
3. Name, email, phone, answers, form ID, campaign attribution, and the exact consent snapshot are stored once and deduplicated.
4. Ryan receives an immediate owner alert.
5. The applicant receives an immediate application confirmation through Resend.
6. If the form's email checkbox is checked, the applicant enters the 30-day sequence: one email per day, days 1 through 30.
7. Every sequence email has one-click unsubscribe headers and a visible unsubscribe link.
8. The completion screen sends the applicant to the owned `/free-build` funnel.

## Offer Recommendation

### 1. Best Offer

Lead with the approved five-page website at a $0 build fee, then use the form to
identify the one paid system the business actually needs next. The first paid
tool offer is the $97 Tool Blueprint. Finished focused tools start at $497; a
tool plus landing page, lead form, alerts, and analytics is $997.

### 2. Why This Offer Fits

The free website removes the largest first objection and proves delivery. The
two form questions also test product demand and budget readiness instead of
assuming every lead wants the same package.

### 3. Why A Smaller Offer Is Not Enough

Calling a full custom interactive tool a $97 product would create an open-ended
delivery promise. At $97, the buyer receives a real blueprint: inputs, outputs,
logic, lead route, wireframe, and written production quote.

### 4. Why A Bigger Offer Is Too Early Or Appropriate

CRM, searchable archives, advertising, and full company systems can be the
right larger build, but only after the first tool or funnel exposes real usage,
lead quality, and follow-up needs. Those systems remain separately scoped.

### 5. Short CTA

Apply for the free website. Tell me what you want built next.

### 6. Alternate Lower-Priced Path

Choose the free website only, or buy the $97 Tool Blueprint without committing
to a production build or monthly service.
