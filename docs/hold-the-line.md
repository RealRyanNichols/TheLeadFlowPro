# Hold The Line — the second offer lane

Shipped Aug 21, 2026. Lane one sells a business owner a platform they own.
This lane teaches them to keep speaking on it: how to answer critics and
trolls without losing the account, and how to recover if they already did.
Product spec, approved copy, and the email series live in Notion under
**The LeadFlow Pro -> 14. Hold The Line**.

Two hard rules survive into every piece of copy in this lane: we never
research, profile, or publish personal details about a private individual,
and we never guarantee an account gets restored. `tests/hold-the-line.test.ts`
pins the offer data against both, plus the no-em-dash rule.

## The email series wiring

The Resend automation is triggered by the **`hold-the-line-lead`** event, a
new event that is deliberately NOT `free-build-lead`. Reusing that one would
drop these leads into the retired 31-email Free Build series. The Free Build
path is untouched: `enrollInEmailSeries()` now takes a series event name but
defaults to `free-build-lead`, so every existing caller behaves exactly as
before.

Enrollment flow, in order:

1. `/api/hold-the-line/lead` saves the lead (service role when configured),
   checks the buyer table, claims `lead_emails` step 0, fires the event, and
   writes a `lead_activity` note. `lead_activity.kind` has a CHECK constraint
   (`stage_change | owner_change | note | task | system | message | delete`);
   these rows use `system`.
2. The daily followups cron sweeps recent `interest = hold_the_line` leads
   and enrolls anyone the instant path missed. Same claim-first contract:
   step 0 in `lead_emails` is the enrollment marker, and
   `UNIQUE (lead_id, step)` means a lead can never be enrolled twice.
3. On a paid `hold-the-line-playbook` checkout the webhook records the
   purchase, emails the playbook link, fires **`hold-the-line-purchase`**,
   and stamps step 0 onto every matching lead row. Both enrollment paths
   also check `purchases` directly, so a buyer never enters the nurture,
   and the automation's exit rule pulls out anyone already in it.

repo and is not committed here). With the bundle in hand:

   (`claude/resend-daily-rebuild.md`): name from manifest, From
   `Ryan Nichols <hello@theleadflowpro.com>`, subject before preview text,
   upload to the FIRST file-input ref, publish past the two expected
   warnings.
2. Build the automation from `automation-definition.json`: trigger
   `hold-the-line-lead`, 63 steps (32 sends, 31 delays), template IDs from
   the mentionTemplates query in two passes. Stop the automation before
   saving. Add the exit rule on **`hold-the-line-purchase`** so buyers leave
   the series.
3. Confirm the chain in the editor, press Start, then send a test lead
   through `/hold-the-line` and watch Runs climb.
4. Do not touch the live Free Build automation
   `019fe95f-95a1-74f8-90a3-6f0f772396e2`.

Until the automation exists, leads still save, Ryan still gets the internal
alert, and the fired events are simply unheard. Nothing breaks; the series
just has not started.
