## The diagnosis

The business has a lead problem in the opposite direction from the one it
has been solving. Ads work: 47 people asked for a free website in thirty
days. The software answers every one of them by email within minutes. Then
nothing happens. A person did not call 46 of them. No proposal was sent. No
card was charged. Revenue is zero because the step between "lead" and
"customer" is a human conversation and that step has no owner, no list, and
no clock.

Everything else on the list is real but secondary:

- **The plugin** is not "in" ChatGPT or Claude because neither directory
  lists it yet. It already installs by address on every paid plan of both.
  A listing is a distribution channel; it will not produce the first
  customer, and both directories need Ryan's own verified organisation.
- **Search** cannot find the business because no page said where it was or
  what it sold in the words a buyer types, and the name collides with an
  unrelated app called LeadFlowPro.
- **Email** is on a GoDaddy-managed Microsoft tenant with an SPF record that
  does not include Microsoft, so the replies Ryan types by hand may be
  landing in spam.
- **DigitalOcean** is a server with no job. Moving the site there would
  cost weeks and add nothing.

So the plan is: call the people who already asked. Make that impossible to
forget. Then make it easier for the next person to book. Then get found.

## What shipped today (code, merged at a green checkpoint)

1. **The call sheet.** `/admin/call-sheet` lists every open lead no person
   has called, texted, or noted, in order: people who wrote to us and are
   waiting, then new leads under three days old, then older ones, then
   open leads that went quiet for five days. Each row has one-tap call,
   text (only with consent), email, and "open and log the call". The
   welcome email and the automatic text do not count as a touch. A morning
   email of the same sheet exists at `/api/cron/call-sheet`, off until Ryan
   sets `CALL_SHEET_EMAIL_ENABLED=true`. The sales desk's Today queue at
   `/admin/sales` is now linked from the admin bar too.
2. **Texting made safe to switch on.** STOP is now checked in the one
   function every automated text goes through, not only on the Meta path,
   and automated texts hold outside 8 am to 9 pm Central. A text a person
   sends from the CRM is not held. The consultation text-back now names
   the consultation and promises one business day, the same as the page
   and the email. The switch itself (`QUO_OUTBOUND_SMS_DISABLED`) stays off
   until Ryan flips it.
3. **One-tap texting on the site.** Every "call or text" link is now a call
   link and a separate text link with a prefilled first line, including a
   text button in the phone header outside the menu.
4. **Booking link, ready for the calendar.** `bookingPage` in
   `lib/site/external-links.ts` is empty. The moment Ryan pastes a Google
   Calendar appointment page into it, the confirmation screen, the welcome

5. **Search.** The homepage, services page, and six agency pages now carry
   Longview and East Texas in their titles in the words buyers type. A
   `/longview` landing page exists, built from the same config as the rest

6. **Plugin directory readiness.** Verification routes for the ChatGPT

7. **Runbooks.** `docs/infrastructure/google-workspace-migration.md` and

## The daily rhythm that makes it work

- 7:30 am: the call sheet. Call before you read anything else.
- After every call: one note on the lead, and a status change if it moved.
- Tuesday and Thursday afternoons: proposals for anyone who said yes to a
  sit-down.
- Friday: the Monday digest is automatic; read the call sheet's counts
  instead. If "Answer now" is not empty on Friday afternoon, that is the
  week's problem, not the ads.

## What is not in this plan, on purpose

- No new ad spend, no new campaign. The ads are producing more leads than
  are being called.
- No migration of the site to DigitalOcean.
- No new product. Nine engines shipped last week; none of them has a
  customer yet.
- No claim of graduates, placements, rankings, or results. The scoreboard
  and the approved-claims registry stay the only sources of numbers.
