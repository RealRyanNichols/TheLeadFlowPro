# OA01 The ChatGPT Operator: image prompts

One prompt per lesson plus one course hero. Paste each into ChatGPT and generate at 4K. Every prompt is 16:9, no text anywhere in the image, no faces unless generic and natural, a real workspace or East Texas small business setting, warm off-white and cobalt accents, nothing futuristic, no robots. If a result shows lettering, a screen with readable words, or a strange hand, regenerate with "same image, remove the lettering" or "same image, hands out of frame."

On the desk: an open laptop showing a clean blank document with only faint layout blocks, a single printed one-page sheet, a cobalt blue folder, a cobalt pen, a coffee mug, reading glasses. Warm off-white walls and desk surface. No people, no faces, no readable text on any screen or paper, no logos. Calm, grounded, operator at work, nothing futuristic.
